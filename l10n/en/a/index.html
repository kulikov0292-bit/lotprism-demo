      Why this product is worth checking

      Every reason has a source, and its explanation opens when you select it. No single indicator proves on its own that the product is a good fit, so the card has no overall score.

        This is a lower bound on purchases for a single listing over the past month: not exact sales, not a forecast and not a LotPrism calculation. It should not be multiplied by the price.

        It reflects demand for this listing, not for your offer at your price. If there is no badge, demand is unknown, not zero.

      2. 02FACTcompetition5 sellers, none of them AmazonKeepa · 64 observations over 90 days

        When Amazon is not among the sellers, price pressure is usually lower, but this is not proof. The mix of sellers may change.

      3. 03FACTprice over 90 days€23.90 – €25.90, now €24.90amazon.de price history · chart

        A narrow price range makes the maximum purchase price calculation more stable. The forecast on the chart is produced by a model and is not used for that calculation or to determine the verdict.

      1. 01FACTdemand signal6 similar sets: 200+ to 500+ bought, 1–3 sellers per setAmazon badges via Keepa · selected by the LotPrism team · 26/09/2026

        The team selects amazon.de listings that show the ‘bought in past month’ badge and have few sellers. Each badge is a lower bound for its own listing; they cannot be added together.

        This is demand for existing sets. Demand for your set remains a hypothesis.

      Return on purchase price – what remains of the listing price after VAT, fees, shipping to Amazon and the purchase itself, as a percentage of the purchase price. This is not margin: margin is calculated on revenue. Levels available in the product: 0 / 10 / 20 / 30%.

      Which Amazon marketplaces does LotPrism work with?

      One marketplace is currently connected – Amazon Germany (amazon.de): marketplace data, prices in EUR, 19% VAT. Other Amazon marketplaces are not connected yet.

      How are products for resale selected?

      Marketplace data is collected automatically. The selection comes from screening this data against set thresholds; at the moment, the LotPrism team runs the screening. For each product, the software shows the reasons, the calculated maximum purchase price and the next step.

      How does a supplier price list get into LotPrism?

      The LotPrism team uploads price lists; supplier feeds update automatically. LotPrism matches price list items to amazon.de listings by EAN/GTIN and units per pack. You cannot upload a price list yourself in the app yet.

      Do you need a price list to get started?

      No. The card is useful even without a supplier: it has the reasons, the calculated maximum purchase price and the next step. A price list refines the purchase assessment.

      Can you start research directly from the card?

      Not yet. At the moment, the LotPrism team carries out supplier search and demand research; the offers it finds appear on the product card. You will be able to start research from the card once this action is available.

      Are there any forecasts?

      For products with a price history on Amazon, there is a 30-day price forecast with a P10–P90 range. The forecast is research-only and is not grounds for buying: it is not used to calculate the maximum purchase price or to determine the verdict. There is no unit-sales forecast.

      Does LotPrism check product quality?

      Not in the current version. LotPrism matches the EAN/GTIN and units per pack against the Amazon listing. Product quality, samples and approval to sell the brand are for you to check.

      Does LotPrism buy products itself?

      No. In the current version, the software does not place orders or contact suppliers. The decision to buy and the order are up to you.

      Where does LotPrism use AI?

      In two places. A language model helps the team search for suppliers and research demand for a new listing. A machine learning model produces the price forecast. The verdict and the maximum purchase price are calculated by rules based on marketplace data; LotPrism has no chat.
